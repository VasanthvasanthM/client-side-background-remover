Remove the background from an Etsy listing photo

Open the tool

Free, no signup, and your image never leaves your device.

Etsy does not mandate white, but a consistent background across a shop is what makes a listing page look like a shop rather than a collection of snapshots.

Why these settings

Automatic mode, then whichever background colour you have standardised on. Because changing the colour does not recompute the cut, doing a whole batch to one colour is quick.

Step by step

  • Drop an Etsy listing photo onto the page, paste it, or choose the file.
  • Apply the settings above — they are a starting point, not a rule.
  • Check the edges at full size, and fix anything left with the brush.
  • Choose transparent or a solid colour, then download.

What to watch out for

Handmade items often have fuzzy or irregular edges — wool, paper, raw wood. Keep the feather on and avoid a negative edge shift, which would trim exactly the texture that sells the item.

Common questions

What is the best way to remove the background from an Etsy listing photo?

Use the settings above as a starting point, then check the edges at full size. Most of the work is choosing the right mode; the sliders only fine-tune it.

Is an Etsy listing photo uploaded to a server?

No. It is decoded and processed entirely in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. There is no server to send it to.

Is there a watermark or a limit?

Neither. No watermark is added, there is no cap on how many images you process, and no account is needed.
