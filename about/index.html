About

Open the tool

Free, no signup, and your image never leaves your device.

What this is

Remove the background from an image and download a transparent PNG. Everything runs in your browser — your picture is never uploaded. Free, no signup, no watermark.

Why it runs in your browser

Most free tools of this kind upload your file to a server, process it there, and ask you to trust a privacy policy. This one does the work in the browser you are already using. Your file never travels, there is no queue, no size limit set by someone else’s server, and no copy left behind on a machine you do not control.

Who makes it

This site is built and maintained by Vasanth M, an independent developer in Trichy, Tamil Nadu, India. It is one of a small family of browser-based tools built on the same principle: do the work locally, charge nothing, and explain honestly how it works.

How it is paid for

Nothing is sold here. There is no paid tier, no account and no upsell.

Languages

Every page is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Hindi, Indonesian and Arabic — translated, not machine-switched at load.

Getting in touch

Corrections, bug reports and feature requests are welcome at vasanthm996@gmail.com. A report that names the browser and what you were doing is worth ten that do not.
