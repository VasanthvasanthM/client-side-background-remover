When this works well

A flat or near-flat blue area that is clearly different from your subject. Compression noise and lighting variation are expected — that is what the tolerance absorbs. Where it struggles is a subject containing the same blue, and there the brush is faster than any amount of slider tuning.

Common questions

What tolerance should I use for a blue background?

Start at 42 and adjust from there. Raise it if flecks of blue survive; lower it if the subject starts disappearing.

It removed part of my subject as well. What now?

The subject contains something close to that blue. Lower the tolerance until the subject is safe, accept the leftover specks, and remove those with the brush — that is reliably faster than finding a tolerance that does both.

Does it remove blue inside the subject too?

Only if you want it to. Leave connectivity on and only the area joined to the edges goes; turn it off and every matching pixel goes, including enclosed gaps.
